Privacy policy

In short

V-Reseller Dashboard runs 100% locally, in your browser. Your sales, purchase prices and exports are never sent to a server run by the publisher, and the publisher does not operate one.

Data controller

Simon RIHOUEY LEREBOURG (EI), contact: contact.vreseller@gmail.com.

Data processed by the Extension

  • Vinted sales data (items, prices, dates, statuses, fees, wallet transactions): read from your Vinted account, in your browser, only to produce your dashboards and exports. It never leaves your computer.
  • Settings and purchase prices you enter: saved in the browser's local storage (chrome.storage). You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the Extension.
  • Exported files (Excel, FEC): created directly on your computer.
  • Licence key (paid plans): sent to Gumroad only to check that it is valid.

The Extension contains no analytics, no advertising trackers, and sells no data.

When you buy, your name, email and payment details are processed by Gumroad, Inc. under its own privacy policy (gumroad.com/privacy). The publisher receives your email and order details from Gumroad. They are used to manage your licence, support and accounting obligations, and are kept for the legal retention period for accounting records (10 years).
